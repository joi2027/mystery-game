The English Room

Day 1: English Class

You have found the english room and you have fond memories. This was your class room. You feel the walls and remember the kids' laughter, jokes and fun. You miss them but most of them are gone and dead. Crying in the silence of the night you came across a vent. You don't really remember it being there but it seems to be the only way out. Till you look through the door and see a black void on the other side it looks to be the basement. Which one do you choose?
